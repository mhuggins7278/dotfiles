---
name: grilling
description: Stress-test a plan, decision, or design through focused rounds of questions. Use when a workflow needs shared understanding before action or when the user asks to grill an idea.
---

Interview the user until the plan, decision, or design has a shared
understanding. Map it as a design tree: each decision branches into the
decisions that depend on it.

Work in rounds. The frontier is every decision whose prerequisites are settled
and can be answered now. Ask the whole frontier in one numbered round, include
a recommended answer for each question, then wait for the user's answers before
recomputing the next frontier.

Ask only questions whose answers materially change behavior, architecture,
safety, cost, or scope. Do not explore theoretical branches that cannot affect
the work.

Facts are the agent's job. Inspect the environment or delegate focused
exploration instead of asking the user for facts that can be looked up. The
user owns decisions and preferences; do not answer those on the user's behalf.

The session is complete when no material decision remains unresolved. Do not act
on the plan until the user confirms that shared understanding has been reached.
